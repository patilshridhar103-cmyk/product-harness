# PRI-SYN-001 — Prioritization Synthesis

**Project:** Smart Prioritization (PRI) · **Type:** Synthesis · **Date:** 2026-08-12
**Skill:** researcher · **Evidence:** 12 customer interviews (Aug 2026, fresh)

---

## Top Pain Point: Prioritization overhead
**Signal:** HIGH · **Frequency:** 9/12 (75%) · **Quantified:** ~4 hrs/week in spreadsheets

- Sarah Chen (SaaS, 50p): "I spend 4 hours a week in spreadsheets."
- Alex Patel (SaaS, 200p): "2-hour meeting every sprint just to rank features."
- Jordan (SaaS, 20p): "A shared Google sheet nobody trusts."
- [6 more consistent mentions]

## Secondary: Can't see ROI on features
**Signal:** MEDIUM · 6/12 interviews. Customers ship requested features that go unused.

## Contradiction flagged
Two customers framed the pain as "too many meetings," not "no tool" — is the root
cause *lack of structure* or *lack of business-value clarity*? → became Assumption A-1.

## Missing evidence
No mobile-workflow data; no churn linkage. Data all <1 month old.

## Top finding
Prioritization is a real, quantified, high-frequency pain for 75% of customers →
strong candidate for opportunity generation.
