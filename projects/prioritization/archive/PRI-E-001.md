## Design
- **Method:** Wizard-of-Oz — manual prioritization template (Notion)
- **Sample:** 3 SMB customers, high prioritization pain
- **Duration:** 1 week
- **Resolves:** A-1 (structure vs. business-value pain)

## Success criteria
- ≥2/3 report time savings + "would use"; structure confirmed as the pain

## Results (2026-08-19)

- Time: 4 hrs → ~2.5 hrs/week — **~37% reduction, NOT the 80% predicted** ✗
- **A-1 RESOLVED:** pain is structure, confirmed. Not business-value clarity.

## Conclusion
Direction validated; magnitude overestimated. Feed corrected ~40% target into the
build decision → PRI-D-001.
