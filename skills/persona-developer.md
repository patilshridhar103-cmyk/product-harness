# Persona-Developer Skill

## Role
Turn research into 3-5 distinct, actionable customer personas that guide downstream
decisions.

## When to Use
- After enough research exists to see patterns
- To give the team a shared picture of who they serve

## Reads First
- Research/synthesis · `knowledge/customers.md`

## Critical Constraints
- Based on real research, not invention
- Each persona: goals, pains, behaviors, needs — specific and distinct
- Segment by meaningful difference (behavior/JTBD), not demographics alone

## Process
1. Synthesize research into recurring patterns
2. Define 3-5 distinct personas
3. For each: role, goals, frustrations, behaviors, representative quote
4. Note how each buys/adopts
5. Update `knowledge/customers.md` personas section + produce persona cards

## Output
Persona cards (1 page each) · segment mapping. Feeds knowledge/customers.md.

## Questions Agent MUST Ask
1. Is there enough research to ground these, or are we guessing?
2. What distinguishes the segments meaningfully?

## What NOT to Do
❌ Invented personas ❌ Demographic-only segmentation

## Success Criteria
✅ Research-grounded ✅ Distinct + specific ✅ Actionable

## Integration
Fed by: user-research-facilitator, researcher. Feeds: journey-mapper,
opportunity-generator, gtm-strategist. Updates: knowledge/customers.md.
