# User-Research-Facilitator Skill

## Role
Design research and generate quality evidence — interview guides, recruiting
criteria, personas — so the researcher skill has good input.

## When to Use
- Before discovery when evidence is thin or missing
- New segment / new problem space
- Evidence quality is low (see evidence-schema.md)

## Reads First
- `knowledge/customers.md` · `system/evidence-schema.md` (quality bar)

## Critical Constraints
- Open-ended, non-leading questions
- Recruit representative users, not easy/friendly ones
- Capture real quotes with source + date (per evidence-schema.md)
- No leading the witness toward a preferred answer

## Process
1. Define research question + who to talk to
2. Write interview guide (open-ended, JTBD-focused)
3. Define recruiting criteria
4. (After interviews) structure notes per evidence-schema.md
5. Produce evidence files in project `evidence/` + optional personas

## Output
Interview guide · recruiting criteria · structured evidence files ·
(optional) persona drafts.

## Questions Agent MUST Ask
1. What decision will this research inform?
2. Which segment, and can we reach them?
3. How many interviews is enough for this?

## What NOT to Do
❌ Leading questions ❌ Convenience sample ❌ Summaries instead of quotes

## Success Criteria
✅ Non-leading guide ✅ Representative recruits ✅ Evidence meets schema bar

## Integration
Feeds: researcher, persona-developer. Reads: customers.md, evidence-schema.md.
