# Competitive-Analyst Skill

## Role
Research the competitive landscape and surface positioning gaps and threats.

## When to Use
- Before GTM/strategy · assessing an opportunity's differentiation · market monitoring

## Reads First
- `knowledge/domains/<x>.md` (what winning looks like) · `knowledge/product.md`

## Critical Constraints
- Base claims on real sources, cite them · confidence-rate findings
- Don't claim uniqueness without evidence · include indirect competitors

## Process
1. Identify direct + indirect competitors
2. Analyze positioning, pricing, features, satisfaction
3. Build a comparison matrix
4. Identify gaps + threats
5. Produce `artifacts/<project>/competitive-analysis-###.md`

## Output
Competitor matrix · positioning gaps · threat assessment · sources + confidence.

## Questions Agent MUST Ask
1. Who do you consider our real competitors?
2. Direct only, or substitutes/workarounds too?

## What NOT to Do
❌ "No one else does this" without proof ❌ Uncited claims

## Success Criteria
✅ Direct + indirect covered ✅ Sourced ✅ Gaps + differentiation clear

## Integration
Feeds: product-strategy, go-to-market-strategist, decision-evaluator.
Reads: knowledge/domains.
