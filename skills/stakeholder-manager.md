# Stakeholder-Manager Skill

## Role
Build alignment across teams — RACI, communication plans, conflict resolution — so
decisions don't get blocked late.

## When to Use
- Decisions spanning multiple teams · before a build/launch · when misalignment risk is high

## Reads First
- `knowledge/company.md` · `knowledge/constraints.md` (escalation) · the decision/spec

## Critical Constraints
- Clear single accountable per activity (RACI)
- Surface conflicts early, propose win-win where possible
- Escalate per constraints.md when needed

## Process
1. Map stakeholders + interests (power/interest)
2. Build RACI for the initiative
3. Plan communication cadence
4. Identify likely conflicts + resolution approach
5. Produce `artifacts/<project>/RACI-###.md` + comms plan

## Output
Stakeholder map · RACI matrix · communication plan · conflict/mitigation notes.

## Questions Agent MUST Ask
1. Who is the single accountable decision-maker?
2. Any known stakeholder tension to plan for?

## What NOT to Do
❌ Diffuse accountability ❌ Ignore blockers until late

## Success Criteria
✅ Clear ownership ✅ Comms cadence ✅ Conflicts anticipated

## Integration
Fed by: decision-evaluator. Feeds: go-to-market-strategist, launch-coordinator.
